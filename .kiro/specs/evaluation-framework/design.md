# Effectiveness evaluation: design

prepare freezes the protocol. The launcher creates/verifies a private PostgreSQL target; run_trial ingests normalized records, records baseline retrieval, invokes Dream Cycle and writes the local briefing/evidence bundle.

Shuffled packets hide the retrieval/discovery arm during review. record_review appends 0–4/null judgments and observations. Native acquisition, distillation, external delivery and human benefit require separate evidence.
